Free addon · AutoFisher

Fish with one key.

Press F to cast. When the bobber splashes, press F again to reel in. Your catch is looted automatically, and the splash is loud enough to hear over everything else.

Download AutoFisher Version 1.0.0 · zip · 6 KB · Release notes

Install

  1. Download the zip above.
  2. Unzip it into your game's Interface/AddOns folder, so you have AddOns/AutoFisher/AutoFisher.lua.
  3. Restart the game. If the AddOns list says it's out of date, tick Load out of date AddOns.

Fish

  1. Type /af to turn fishing mode on.
  2. Press F to cast your line.
  3. Press F on the splash to reel in. The loot goes straight to your bags.
  4. Type /af again when you're done. Your settings go back to normal.

What it does while you fish

  • One keyCasts when the line is in, reels in when it's out
  • Loud splashMusic, ambience and dialog muted, effects at full volume
  • Auto lootTurned on while fishing
  • Settings restoredOn stop, on logout, or on your next login after a crash
  • Your key backF is only taken over while fishing mode is on
  • Combat safeIf a fight interrupts you, the key goes back to casting afterwards
  • Catch logCounts what you've caught this session and in total

Commands

/afTurn fishing mode on or off
/af key GUse a different key: any key, like G, SHIFT-F or BUTTON4. The default is F.
/af sound on / offLoud splash while fishing (on by default)
/af loot on / offAuto loot while fishing (on by default)
/af statsWhat you've caught this session and in total
/af resetClear the catch log

Game versions

WoW: Forever, Retail, Classic Era, TBC Anniversary and Mists Classic: everything works, including pressing your key on the splash to reel in.

Older clients (such as 3.3.5 servers): the key casts, and you right-click the bobber to reel in.

If pressing the key on the splash doesn't reel in, right-clicking the bobber always works.

Why one key, not zero

Blizzard only lets an addon cast a spell or click the bobber when you press a key or click. Programs that fish with nobody at the keyboard are bots, and accounts that use them get banned.

AutoFisher stays within the rules: every cast and every catch is one press of your key.

Release notes

1.0.0Latest7 November 2026 · first release

New

  • One-key fishing. Type /af to turn on fishing mode. Your key (F by default) casts. While your line is in the water, the same key reels in the bobber.
  • Loud splash. While you fish, music, ambience and dialog are muted and sound effects play at full volume.
  • Auto loot. Auto loot is turned on while you fish.
  • Catch log. /af stats shows what you've caught this session and in total. /af reset clears it.
  • Your own key. /af key G sets any key, including SHIFT-F or a mouse button like BUTTON4.
  • On-screen hint. A line near the top of the screen tells you what your key does right now.

Safety

  • Every setting AutoFisher changes is put back when you stop or log out. If the game closes unexpectedly, it puts them back on your next login.
  • Your key is only taken over while fishing mode is on. Turn it off and your normal binding comes back.
  • If combat interrupts you, the key goes back to casting once the fight ends.
  • AutoFisher never casts or loots on its own. Every cast and every catch is one press of your key.

Known limitations

  • On older clients (such as 3.3.5 servers), the key casts and you right-click the bobber to reel in.
  • The game may list AutoFisher as "out of date". Tick Load out of date AddOns in the AddOns list.
  • If pressing your key on the splash doesn't reel in, right-clicking the bobber always works.
